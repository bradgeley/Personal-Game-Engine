Money
- Towers cost money to buy
- Interest is accumulated each round on money you don't spend (5% base)

Debt
- You can go into debt to buy towers
- Interest is accumulated each round on debt you owe
- You must pay off debt by the end of the game or you lose
- Credit Limit: max amount of debt
- Debt Interest Rate (20% base) - separate from regular interest rate (5%)
	- Can be modified by roguelike modifiers
- Higher debt == more/stronger enemies come, so you do get higher risk/higher reward for spending money, even money you don't have
	- Need to balance it so its about as good as allowing compounding interest to multiply your money
